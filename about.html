About ECOGROWTH LTD

Professional greenhouse construction, built on practical experience and honest workmanship.

OUR STORY

Rooted In Practical Construction

ECOGROWTH LTD specializes in greenhouse construction, installation, shade-net structures and maintenance for farmers and agricultural businesses across Kenya. We work directly with clients to design structures that fit their land, their crops and their budget.

Every project is approached with the same focus: sound structural planning, quality materials and installation that is built to last through Kenya's varied growing conditions.

HOW WE WORK

A Straightforward Process

1. Consultation

We discuss your space, crop plans and budget to understand what you need.

2. Design & Quotation

We recommend a structure and provide a transparent, itemized quotation.

3. Construction & Installation

Our team builds and installs the structure with attention to structural quality.

WHAT GUIDES US

Practical, Honest, Built To Last

  • Professional workmanship on every project
  • Transparent, itemized pricing
  • Structures designed around client needs
  • Responsive communication throughout the project
